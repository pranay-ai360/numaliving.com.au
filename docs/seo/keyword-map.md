# Keyword map

Which search queries each page is meant to answer, the real buyer questions behind them, and draft titles and descriptions. It comes from the US search study of 18 October 2026 ([`competitor-analysis-2026-10.md`](competitor-analysis-2026-10.md)). The URLs are the **proposed** map in [ADR 0003](../adr/0003-us-seo-strategy.md): they become final when the owner signs the map off. There are no search volumes or difficulty scores behind this (no keyword tool was available); the order is a reading of the results pages.

Wave A pages are launch-critical (about 16 pages); Wave B pages follow in the first 90 days, two to three a month.

## Real question bank

Seen in Google's People also ask on 18 October 2026. Each is meant as a question heading on a page of the matching topic, with a 40 to 60 word answer.

- **Cost:** How much does custom millwork cost? Why is custom millwork so expensive? Is custom millwork expensive?
- **Definition:** What is a millwork manufacturer? What does custom millwork mean? What is considered architectural millwork? Is millwork rough or finish carpentry? Why do they call it millwork? What is the difference between architectural woodwork and millwork? What are some examples of architectural millwork? Are custom cabinets millwork?
- **Standards:** What is AWI premium grade? What is AWI in millwork? What are the AWI millwork standards? What is an AWI certification?
- **Importing:** Are kitchen cabinets subject to tariffs? What is the HS code for cabinets?
- **Multifamily:** What division is millwork in construction? Who are the largest multifamily builders in the US?
- **Fluted panels:** Where can I buy fluted wall panels? Which fluted panel is best? What are the disadvantages of fluted panels? Are fluted wall panels still in style?
- **Brand:** What happened to the USA millwork? (Millwork USA built its answer.) Is Numa a legit company? What does Numa stand for?
- **Related searches worth a page or a section:** AWI premium grade vs custom grade; AWI certified millwork contractors; Architectural Woodwork Standards PDF; import custom millwork from overseas cost.
