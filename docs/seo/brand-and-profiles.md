# Brand and profiles

This document sets the naming rules for Numa Living, shows the other businesses that share the name, gives a trademark-check checklist for the owner and a US trademark attorney, holds the profile copy deck, the name-address-phone rules, and the steps to publish profile links in the site's structured data. It is the companion to [entity-and-links.md](entity-and-links.md), which says where the profiles and links go and when.

Status: draft for owner review, 18 Oct 2026

Section 4 is a checklist, not legal advice. Use it with a US-licensed trademark attorney.

## 1. The name

1. **The name is "Numa Living"**: capital N, capital L, one space. The same in page text, titles, profile names, listings and structured data.
2. **The wordmark is a logo.** The stacked "NUMA / LIVING" on the site is a logo treatment. In running text and form fields write "Numa Living", as the footer already does ("© 2026 Numa Living").
3. **Do not shorten it in names and titles.** "Numa" alone is the name of many other businesses (section 3). Inside a sentence, after the full name has been used, "Numa" is fine; the site's own About text does this.
4. **No company suffix by default.** Add "LLC", "FZE" or similar only where the owner supplies the legal name and the field is for it.
5. **One descriptor.** It follows the name: `Numa Living | <descriptor>` in titles and taglines, a comma in sentences. Choose it once (section 2).
6. **One handle.** Use the same handle on every platform. If the plain `numaliving` is taken, pick one fixed variant and use it wherever it is free. Do not use a different variant per platform. Check a handle by opening the profile URL in a browser; whether to create the account is the owner's call.
7. **The group line.** Write "A Conqueror Group company" exactly as the site does, or leave it out. Do not link to any Conqueror website until the owner confirms which entity is the parent and a public page names Numa Living.
8. **No certification wording without a document.** ISO, FSC, AWI, QCP, CARB and TSCA do not appear in profiles until the document exists. The site itself marks ISO 9001 and FSC "(to be confirmed)".
9. **US spelling and words.** Paneling, closets, millimeter, schedule, general contractors. This matches the vocabulary pass planned for the site (Phase 2). The site today says "joinery", "panelling", "wardrobes", "programme" and "main contractors", so profiles stay unpublished until the site is changed (gate G3 in [entity-and-links.md](entity-and-links.md)).

## 4. Trademark-check checklist (not legal advice)

This is a checklist for the owner and a US trademark attorney. It is not legal advice and it does not replace counsel.

**What the USPTO says** (checked 18 Oct 2026):

- Its database covers "federally registered and applied-for trademarks". The examining attorney's search "will only determine whether there are conflicting trademarks in our federal database", and "It's your responsibility to search state trademark databases and the internet". A comprehensive clearance search covers the federal database, state databases and the internet. (https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/why-search-similar-trademarks)
- The search tool is the "Trademark Search system" at tmsearch.uspto.gov. (https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search)
- An applicant or registrant whose domicile is outside the United States must be represented by a US-licensed attorney. The rule took effect on 3 August 2019. A domicile address is required, a P.O. box is generally not acceptable as one, and not appointing an attorney "may result in a loss of legal rights". (https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/laws/trademark-rule-requires-foreign-applicants-and-registrants-have-us)

**Checklist.**

1. **Fix the mark.** The word mark "Numa Living". Decide whether "NUMA" alone or the logo also matters. The descriptor is not part of the mark.
2. **Describe the goods and services in plain words.** Custom millwork; kitchens, closets and vanities; wall and ceiling paneling; doors; reception desks and other statement pieces; loose furniture (FF&E); design, engineering, manufacture and installation support. Counsel maps these to classes.
3. **Search the federal register.** Use the USPTO Trademark Search. Try "NUMA LIVING", "NUMA" alone, "NUMA" with each word seen in section 3 (CREATIONS, STYLE, STAYS, HOMES, RESERVE, NEST) and sound-alikes. Look in the classes for furniture, wood products, construction and design services. Counsel confirms the classes.
4. **Search state registers and the internet.** The USPTO says this is on the applicant. Counsel searches state registers. The owner and counsel search the web, social handles, app stores, business registries and domain names (.com, .us) for NUMA used in the same fields.
5. **Look first at the known conflicts** from section 3: Numa Creations (same trade, US), Numa Style and the Argentine maker (furniture), Numa Stays (hospitality).
6. **Record every hit** in a small table: name, owner, goods and services, where it trades, registered or in use, URL, date checked, and counsel's view of the risk.
7. **Engage a US-licensed attorney before filing anything.** The USPTO requires one for a foreign-domiciled applicant. Ask counsel also about filing routes outside the US. We researched none.
8. **Decide and record.** Go; go with the descriptor; adjust; or rename. Record the decision in a short ADR, as [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) asks for big decisions, and update section 2.
9. **Time it.** Do this before creating more than two profiles, and before the site opens to search engines. A rename changes every profile and the structured data.
10. **Keep evidence.** Dated screenshots of each search.

## 6. Name, address and phone rules

These keep every profile, listing and the site saying the same thing.

1. **One source of truth.** `site.contact` in [src/config/site.ts](../../src/config/site.ts), as [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) says. Profiles copy from it, never the other way round. When it changes, change this deck first, then each profile, and record the date.
2. **Name.** Exactly "Numa Living".
3. **Address.** Write the factory address once, in a fixed format, and paste it. Choose either "UAE" or "United Arab Emirates" and use it everywhere. Do not use a virtual office, mailbox or P.O. box as an address. Say where the factory is, or leave the address out of profiles that do not require it.
4. **Phone.** One main number in international format with the country code and the same spacing everywhere. If a US number exists, label each number ("US sales", "Factory") and keep the same main number in every profile. WhatsApp uses the same digits as `site.contact.whatsapp`.
5. **Email.** projects@numaliving.us everywhere. No personal addresses on public profiles.
6. **Website.** https://numaliving.us/ exactly. No tracking parameters on profile links: read traffic by referrer in analytics instead, so each profile has one clean URL in [link-log.csv](link-log.csv) and in `sameAs`.
7. **Hours.** One set, with the time zone named.
8. **Descriptions.** Copy from section 5. If a field is shorter, shorten the deck version first.
9. **Images.** The same logo files everywhere.
10. **Placeholders.** Never publish a profile with the site's placeholder phone ("+971 4 000 0000") or address ("Factory address, Dubai, UAE"). The launch guard in [src/config/launchGuard.test.ts](../../src/config/launchGuard.test.ts) keeps the site from opening with them; the same rule applies to profiles.
11. **After any change,** search each profile for the old value.

## 7. Accounts and security

- Register profiles with a company mailbox the owner controls, not a personal address. An unpublished alias on numaliving.us fits (mail for the domain is handled by Forward Email; see [docs/RUNBOOK.md](../RUNBOOK.md), section 6, and [ADR 0002](../adr/0002-custom-domain-numaliving-us.md)).
- At least two people at Numa can administer each profile. Record who in the company's password manager, not in this repository.
- Use two-step sign-in where a platform offers it.
- Never put passwords, recovery codes or keys in the repository, the link log or a pull request.
- Remove a person's admin access when they leave.

## 8. Publishing profile links in structured data

**This section depends on Phase 1.** At commit `b2b1986` (main on 18 Oct 2026), [src/config/site.ts](../../src/config/site.ts) has no `social` field and the site outputs no JSON-LD. The programme's Phase 1 (technical SEO foundation) is planned to add `social: []` to the `site` object, build the Organization JSON-LD in `src/lib/seo/`, and fill its `sameAs` from `site.social`. If the implementation differs, find it with `grep -rn "sameAs" src/` and follow what you find.

**The file and the field:** `src/config/site.ts`, the `social` array on the `site` object. The Organization JSON-LD `sameAs` is filled from it.

**Steps after Phase 1 is merged:**

1. Confirm the profile is live and complete, uses the canonical name and descriptor, and links back to https://numaliving.us/.
2. Add its public URL (https, no tracking parameters) to `social` in `src/config/site.ts`. Order: LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram, Pinterest, then the rest. Only profiles Numa owns and can edit.
3. Update the pin for `social` in `src/config/site.test.ts`. Today that test pins only the contact email; Phase 1 is planned to pin the new fields.
4. Run `npm run lint && npm run typecheck && npm run test:coverage`, then ship through the normal pull request ([docs/RUNBOOK.md](../RUNBOOK.md), section 1). Ask the owner before pushing or merging.
5. After the deploy, check the live page: `curl -s https://numaliving.us/ | grep -o '"sameAs":\[[^]]*\]'`. It should list exactly the URLs you added. If the JSON is formatted differently, view the page source and search for `sameAs`.
6. When a profile is closed or lost, remove its URL from `social` and set `lost` in [link-log.csv](link-log.csv).

**What Google says** (checked 18 Oct 2026, https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/organization): no property is required. `sameAs` is "The URL of a page on another website with additional information about your organization". For `name` it advises "the same name and alternateName that you're using for your site name". It does not guarantee any search feature will appear, and the markup belongs on the home page or a single page that describes the organization. So keep `name` equal to "Numa Living" and add `legalName` from the owner's legal name.

**Do not put in `sameAs`:** an empty or placeholder profile, a page about another Numa business, or a page Numa cannot edit.

**Both directions.** Link the profiles from the site footer as well, so the link goes both ways. That is a code change for Phase 1 or 2 and is not made here.
